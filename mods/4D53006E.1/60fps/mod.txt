name: 60 fps
description: The game at 60 frames a second instead of 30. Each frame shows at the next vblank and the game steps 1/60 s a frame; a frame the device doesn't finish in time shows late, and the game slows down with it.
author: hakuX

[default.xbe]
# The vblanks a frame takes (0x554944): the present interval set at
# 0x1DA4A0 (TWO_OR_IMMEDIATE -> ONE_OR_IMMEDIATE) and the frame time the
# game steps by at 0x1D9660 (vblanks / refresh rate, 2/60 -> 1/60 s).
0x00554944: 02 -> 01

[$u\default.xbe]
# Xbox Live's title update (version 0x301): the same variable at 0x555224,
# read at 0x1DAE0B and by the frame time at 0x1D9F80.
0x00555224: 02 -> 01
